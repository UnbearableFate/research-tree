I've finished reading the design files, the shared core, both host adapters, the prompts, rules, docs, demo generator and the two test files. Writing up the findings now.

**Review of batch M5 (03 idea → experiment design)**

The core of the batch is in place and tested through production entry points: the goal anchor and verbatim copy check, the ten hypothesis sections, claim and block caps with pre-written success criteria and failure interpretations, the experiment-design section check, the `sanity` experiment kind that counts toward no seed and is never evaluated, the sanity-and-code-review gate on both submission paths (`update_run(submitted)` and `reserve_submission`), the per-root-cause failure budget with task blocking and attention, the overrun attention, the no-op ablation refusal, and the proxy-wording check. Tests X01–X11 all exist in `DesignTest`, `CostEstimateTest` and `ProxyWordingTest` and exercise the behaviour through the real API. The two hosts do not drift: the Claude `READ_ONLY` regex equals the shared `READ_METHODS`, both `research_review` schemas carry `code_ref`, and the worker, planning and workflow prompts carry the same M5 paragraphs. Config template, schema, rules, README, doc_library and the demo all describe the new contracts. Old databases upgrade by version bump only, and old project records have no `design_required` / `sanity_required` / `code_review_required` so the gates stay off for them.

What is missing or differs from the design:

- **Error code.** 10 §1.6 fixes `DESIGN_SECTION_MISSING` for a hypothesis or experiment design lacking sections; the implementation raises uncoded `ResearchDBError`. Hosts and the Leader cannot route on it.
- **Code-review waiver is unconditional.** 03 §8 lets the Leader release an experiment only when the reviewer was unavailable. `waive_code_review` accepts any current decision document, and `_check_ready` skips the review check entirely once a waiver is set, even if a current review of that commit is `blocking`. A blocking cross-model review can therefore be overridden by a self-authored decision.
- **Milestones.** 03 §10 says `create_experiment` requires `milestone`; it is optional and never read. 03 §6's milestone table, stop/go gates and the root `task_request` additions (baseline families, M0/M1) appear nowhere in rules, doc_library or prompts.
- **Failure budget is hard-coded.** 03 §8 says the budget shares its count with `search.max_repairs_per_root_cause`; the config key is accepted but ignored (`FAILURE_PATCHES = 2` constant).
- Several minor gaps: X09 is enforced only at experiment creation and only against the ablated experiment, not the baseline; a clear review for a commit keeps passing after a later blocking one; `update_hypothesis(doc_id=...)` bypasses the section gate; stale schema header; unused `max_baseline_families` / `proposal_review_rounds` keys; new error codes not documented.
