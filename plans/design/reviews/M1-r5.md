## 评审原文

**Review of batch M1 (final, complete diff): one MAJOR finding, eight MINOR.** I read the four design files, the shared core, the schema, both adapters, the rules and docs, and the test files. I could not run anything (read-only), so "tests pass" is not something I verified.

**What holds up**
- **Recomputation:** `evaluate_experiment` computes every statistic from the registered runs, and `record_verdict` only cross-checks the caller's numbers (`STAT_MISMATCH`).
- **Evaluation records:** they are append-only and bound to runs/attempts, code, harness ref and paths, protocol, metric spec and the references fixed at experiment creation. The basis is re-checked inside the write transaction (`EVIDENCE_CHANGED`).
- **Eligibility:** `get_eligibility`, `go_candidates` and the GO decision share one `_eligibility` path that reads evaluations, not cached labels.
- **Migration:** schema 6 databases are refused for writing until upgraded, and migrated or legacy evaluations cannot support GO.
- **Test ids:** M01–M12, E01–E10 and K08 all exist and go through production entry points; raw database writes are used only to simulate tampering or old records.
- **Host parity:** the read-only allowlists of both adapters equal `READ_METHODS` and are pinned by tests. Worker and planning prompts carry the same contract on both hosts.

**The MAJOR finding**
M05 and the unit check of 05 §2.2 are only half implemented. A fraction-declared metric refuses a percentage value, but a percentage-declared metric with `valid_range: [0, 100]` silently accepts a fraction such as 0.701. That is the range the test fixture and the demo both use, and `doc_library.md` itself says the default range cannot catch it. For a lower-is-better percentage primary this passes the SOTA gate (0.301 against 30.1 reads as +29.8 points). The M05 test covers only the detectable direction.

`plans/design/test-logs/` holds only `M0-baseline.md`. There is no M1 record for either host, so I cannot confirm the Herdr scenario of 14 §4.3. Step 6 of the protocol may write these after this review, so I list it here rather than as a finding.
