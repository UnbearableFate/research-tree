## 评审原文

**Batch M1 review: no CRITICAL findings, 2 MAJOR, 7 MINOR.** I could only read files, so I did not run any test; whether the suite passes is unverified.

The core of the batch matches the design:
- **Recalculation:** statistics are recomputed from registered runs, and `record_verdict` only cross-checks the caller's numbers.
- **Evaluations:** they are append-only and bound to runs/attempts, code, harness, protocol, metric spec and references.
- **Eligibility:** `go_candidates` and the GO write path both go through `_eligibility`, which never reads cached labels.
- **Concurrency:** the write transaction re-checks the signature and raises `EVIDENCE_CHANGED` if anything it rests on moved.
- **Migration:** schema 6→7 is refused for writing until upgraded, and migrated evaluations cannot support GO.
- **Hosts:** both adapters carry the same read-only allowlist, pinned by `tests/test_packaging.py`, and equivalent worker and planning prompts.
- **Test ids:** M01–M12, E01–E10 and K08 all exist and go through production entry points; direct database edits are used only to simulate tampering or old records.

The two MAJOR findings:
- **GO / NOT GO report check can be bypassed.** The decision accepts a `decision`-type document, but only `experiment_result` and `report` documents have their evidence block verified against files. A `decision` document with a fabricated or even malformed block satisfies the check.
- **The artifact-import level is untested.** No test produces `artifact_imported` or exercises `require_artifact_import`; every evaluating test ends at `record_recomputed` or in a refusal.

One MINOR finding rests on my own arithmetic rather than on reading or execution: the gate's behaviour exactly at the threshold.
